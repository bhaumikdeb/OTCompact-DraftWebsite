Please carefully read this information, as your questions are likely answered here.

  • Visit the Before You Apply webpage to learn which states are accepting OTC applications.
  • The application “go live” date to apply for privileges to practice is still to be determined by states that are not yet accepting applications.
  • Each OTC state will open applications at their own pace. The timeline for each state is not set by the OTC administrators. The OTC administrators are working closely with the states to support them through this process.
  • You cannot practice in an OTC state under the OT Compact, even if that state has enacted OTC legislation, until applications open, your state deems you eligible to apply, AND your application is approved.
  • You must apply through the OTC to receive your privileges to practice in OTC states.
  • You do not apply for OTC privileges to practice through state licensing boards.
  • Until you receive your OTC privilege to practice, you need a license to practice in any state you wish to provide OT services. Follow that state licensing board’s traditional licensing process to obtain your license to practice.

What is the Occupational Therapy Compact (OTC)?

The Occupational Therapy Licensure Compact (OT Compact) is an interstate compact, or formal agreement among states, that facilitates interstate practice of occupational therapy. Under the OT Compact, Occupational Therapists (OTs) and Occupational Therapy Assistants (OTAs) who are licensed in good standing in a Compact member state will be eligible to practice in other Compact member states via a “compact privilege,” which is equivalent to a license.

The OT Compact is enacted in some states, meaning those states have passed legislation to be part of the compact (check out the “Maps” page tabs to see which states have joined the OTC). However, the OT Compact is not yet operationalized, meaning the process to apply for and receive compact privileges is still developing.

CompactConnect – A comprehensive data system in development

CompactConnect is a national data system that provides data transfer abilities between states. States are in the process of uploading their licensees’ data into CompactConnect. The OTC will utilize the data system to facilitate issuing privileges to practice to OT practitioners. The OTC application process will occur through CompactConnect. For more information about the process to develop the comprehensive data system, please visit the external page: www.compactconnect.org.

About the OT Compact

The Occupational Therapy Licensure Compact (OT Compact) is an interstate agreement that lets Occupational Therapists (OTs) and Occupational Therapy Assistants (OTAs) practice across member states through a Compact Privilege — equivalent to a full license — instead of applying separately in each one. It also created a shared licensure data system for instant verification of a practitioner’s status, governed by an interstate commission made up of member-state officials. It’s a joint initiative of the American Occupational Therapy Association (AOTA) and the National Board for Certification in Occupational Therapy (NBCOT®). Further reading: Developing the OT Compact (OT Practice Magazine).
